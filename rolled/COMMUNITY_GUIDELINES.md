# Unrolled Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** January 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  

---

## 1. Guiding Philosophy & Mission

Unrolled is designed to facilitate candid, constructive, and open campus discourse. We provide verified university members with an open forum to exchange viewpoints, share lived student experiences, discuss campus developments, and engage in meaningful discussion through designated handles.

Our foundational principle is simple: **Remember the Human.** 

Behind every handle, screen, and submission is a fellow student and community member. While Unrolled shields personal profiles to encourage uninhibited dialogue and authentic expression, our platform is not a consequence-free environment. Access is granted exclusively to verified students, and participation in the community carries an obligation to maintain mutual respect, digital civility, and campus safety.

---

## 2. Core Community Standards

### Rule 1: Harassment, Targeted Defamation & Bullying
Unrolled maintains zero tolerance for harassment, digital intimidation, or targeted bullying. Healthy disagreement and robust debate are encouraged; personal attacks are not.
* **Prohibited Conduct:**
  * Singling out private individuals (peers, classmates, residence staff, or instructional personnel) for ridicule, degradation, or harassment.
  * Explicitly naming private campus individuals in a disparaging, humiliating, or defamatory manner.
  * Directing sustained insults, threats, vulgar mockery, or unwanted sexual commentary toward any individual.
  * Encouraging, coordinating, or inciting others to harass an individual on or off the platform.
* **Acceptable Discourse:**
  * Discussing campus policies, institutional actions, university governance, student government initiatives, or public campus controversies in a respectful and analytical manner.

---

### Rule 2: Hate Speech & Discriminatory Conduct
Every member of our campus community deserves to engage without fear of bias or hostility. We strictly prohibit hate speech in all forms.
* **Prohibited Conduct:**
  * Promoting violence, hatred, dehumanization, or inciting discrimination against individuals or protected groups based on race, ethnicity, caste, national origin, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, sex, disability, or serious medical condition.
  * Utilizing racial, ethnic, misogynistic, homophobic, or ableist slurs, hate symbols, or derogatory tropes.
  * Advocating exclusion, segregation, or inferiority of any marginalized demographic.

---

### Rule 3: Privacy, Confidentiality & Non-Public Personal Information (Anti-Doxxing)
Protecting the privacy and physical security of community members is essential. Publishing another person’s confidential or private information without explicit, verifiable consent is strictly prohibited.
* **Prohibited Conduct:**
  * Disclosing personally identifiable information (PII), including but not limited to: full legal names of private students, personal phone numbers, physical residential addresses, specific dorm rooms, student identification numbers, personal email addresses, or private social media handles.
  * Uploading screenshots of private messages, direct communications, or closed group chats containing non-consenting individuals’ identifiers or personal matters.
  * Revealing or attempting to unmask the private identity behind any handle or pseudonymous profile.
  * Soliciting or offering bounties for personal information or real-world identities.

---

### Rule 4: Physical Safety, Threats of Violence & Self-Harm
Unrolled must remain a safe digital space. Any content that threatens physical well-being or platform safety will result in immediate intervention.
* **Prohibited Conduct:**
  * Expressing intentions, threats, or incitement to commit acts of violence, property destruction, or physical assault.
  * Glorifying, praising, or celebrating violent incidents, mass tragedies, or physical harm inflicted upon others.
  * Encouraging, instructing, or glorifying suicide, self-inflicted injuries, or eating disorders.
* **Crisis Intervention:**
  * Content indicating that a user is experiencing an acute mental health crisis, suicidal ideation, or intent to self-harm will be removed or flagged for immediate safety response, and the user will be directed to available professional crisis resources and campus counseling services.

---

### Rule 5: Non-Consensual Imagery & Sexually Explicit Content
Content of a sexually explicit or non-consensual nature is fundamentally incompatible with the mission of Unrolled.
* **Prohibited Conduct:**
  * Distributing, producing, or soliciting Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Material (CSAM). Any detection results in immediate, permanent platform expulsion and mandatory reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement authorities.
  * Distributing non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), voyeuristic recordings, or imagery captured in private spaces (such as locker rooms, restrooms, or residential dormitories).
  * Soliciting sexual acts, escort services, prostitution, or commercial adult media.
  * Publishing explicit pornography or depictions of graphic sexual acts.

---

### Rule 6: Impersonation, False Representation & Defamation
Authenticity and trust are core values of our platform. Users may not intentionally deceive the community regarding who they are.
* **Prohibited Conduct:**
  * Choosing handles, avatars, or post text intended to mislead others into believing the author is a specific peer, faculty member, administrator, official student organization, or university department.
  * Fabricating malicious falsehoods, libelous claims, or defamatory allegations calculated to inflict reputational or emotional damage on specific campus individuals.

---

### Rule 7: Academic Integrity & Institutional Conduct Codes
Unrolled respects the academic missions of supported universities. The platform must not be used to subvert institutional academic integrity.
* **Prohibited Conduct:**
  * Distributing, requesting, or trading current examination questions, active test materials, unreleased assessments, or unauthorized answer keys.
  * Offering, soliciting, or promoting paid academic cheating services, including contract essay writing, proxy test-taking, or honor code violations.

---

### Rule 8: Platform Integrity, Manipulation & Unsolicited Promotion
The integrity of the Unrolled feed and conversation threads must reflect genuine peer sentiment.
* **Prohibited Conduct:**
  * Engaging in vote manipulation, artificial metrics inflation, coordinated brigading, or mass-flagging campaigns to suppress legitimate viewpoints.
  * Deploying automated scripts, bots, scrapers, or unauthorized third-party clients.
  * Disseminating unsolicited commercial solicitations, multi-level marketing (MLM) schemes, affiliate links, financial scams, or repetitive promotional spam.

---

### Rule 9: Illegal Activities & Controlled Substances
Unrolled strictly forbids the facilitation of criminal activities or the trade of illicit materials.
* **Prohibited Conduct:**
  * Facilitating, promoting, or coordinating the sale, purchase, or distribution of illegal drugs, prescription narcotics without authorization, regulated pharmaceuticals, fraudulent identification documents, or firearms.
  * Organizing, facilitating, or promoting criminal offenses, fraudulent schemes, or hacking activities.

---

## 3. Moderation & Trust Systems

To maintain a secure and constructive environment, Unrolled employs a comprehensive safety architecture combining proactive systems with human review:

1. **Automated Safety Filters:** Real-time linguistic and pattern-detection models scan text submissions upon creation to identify and restrict prohibited terms, hate speech, severe harassment, and phone numbers/addresses prior to dissemination.
2. **Community Reporting:** Community members are empowered to flag content that breaches these guidelines. Reporting options include specific violation categories (e.g., Harassment, Hate Speech, Doxxing, Violence, Spam). Flagged items are escalated immediately to the moderation queue.
3. **Internal Moderation Operations:** Our Trust and Safety team evaluates flagged submissions under uniform criteria, enforcing decisions impartially and transparently.
4. **Member Self-Regulation Tools:** Users maintain direct control over their experience via built-in blocking mechanisms. Blocking another handle immediately hides all submissions from that account across feeds and prevents direct interaction.

---

## 4. Enforcement Ladder & Penalties

Violations of our Community Guidelines result in proportionate enforcement actions based on the severity, intent, and frequency of the violation:

| Enforcement Action | Description & Application |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Content Removal** | The offending post, comment, or media is immediately removed from the community feed. |
| **Formal Safety Warning** | The user account receives an administrative notification detailing the specific guideline breached, serving as an educational corrective measure. |
| **Temporary Cooldown / Suspension** | For severe or repeated infractions, the user's posting and interaction privileges are revoked for a specified duration (e.g., 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days). |
| **Permanent Account Termination** | Severe violations—including credible threats of violence, non-consensual intimate imagery, hate speech, or persistent harassment—result in the immediate and permanent termination of the user account and handle. |
| **Institutional & Legal Escalation** | In cases involving imminent physical danger, credible threats against campus safety, unlawful stalking, or child exploitation, Unrolled reserves the right to notify university administration, campus security, and relevant law enforcement agencies. |

---

## 5. Appeals & Inquiries

We are committed to fair and impartial enforcement. If you believe your content or account was mistakenly penalized, you may submit a formal appeal through our safety portal or contact our Trust and Safety team at:

* **Safety Inquiries, Reports & Appeals:** `support@rolledapp.com`

Please provide your verified university domain, the notification timestamp, and relevant context regarding the action taken. All appeals are reviewed by human safety personnel.
